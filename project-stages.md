# Project stages

This document summarizes the status of a given project, from application


## The levels

| Level | Listed on the site | What happens next |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pre-Seed | No | Applicant reapplies on a stated date. Committee schedules nothing. |
| Seed | Yes, labelled Seed | Mandatory committee review at 6 months. Maximum 12 months at this level. |
| Member | Yes | Light health check at 12 months. |
| Declined | No | 12-month cooling-off. Only when a blocking condition will not be resolved. |

Pre-Seed is a no with a reapplication date. Seed is a yes with the expensive services withheld. Member is what we grant today. Declined becomes rare.

## Blocking conditions are fixable, not fatal

Wrong license, name clash, archived repo, unclear provenance. The application parks until the applicant fixes it, then evaluation restarts on current numbers. OpenMod relicensed to MIT and the review carried on. Declining outright is for the applicant who will not or cannot fix it.

---

## What Seed actually is

A Seed project gets a listing, a named committee mentor, CLA infrastructure, and promotion. It does not get code signing, IP or trademark handling, funds, or the project badge.

At 6 months, the mentor brings the before-and-after numbers to the meeting, and the project is promoted, renewed once, or exited. Nothing sits at Seed longer than 12 months.

Pre-Seed carries no review. The issue is closed and the burden sits with the applicant.
